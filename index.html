BEFORE YOUR APPOINTMENT

INKSUBWAY / STUDIO POLICIES

A little preparation.
A better experience.

Please read Stephanie’s studio policies before arranging your appointment. An enquiry alone does not confirm a booking.

Booking & deposits

01

Deposit required

A 50% non-refundable deposit is required to secure all tattoo appointments.

02

Appointment confirmation

Appointments are only confirmed once the deposit has been received.

03

Deposit policy

Deposits cover appointment reservation and design preparation. Deposits will be forfeited in the event of cancellation, no-show, or failure to attend the scheduled appointment.

04

Rescheduling

Rescheduling requests must be made at least 48 hours before the appointment. One complimentary reschedule may be granted at the artist’s discretion.

Your appointment

05

Arrival time

Arriving late may result in a shortened session, rescheduling, or cancellation of the appointment.

06

Payment

The remaining balance must be paid before the tattoo session begins.

07

Guests

Clients are welcome to bring up to two guests to the studio. Guests must remain respectful of the studio environment and avoid disrupting the tattoo process.

Travel & design

08

Home service

Home service appointments are available upon request and must be arranged in advance. Additional charges apply depending on location, travel distance, and appointment requirements.

09

Changes to design

Any changes to the approved design, size, or placement may affect pricing and appointment duration.

10

Right to refuse service

INKSUBWAY reserves the right to refuse service to anyone who is intoxicated, abusive, or unable to comply with studio policies.

AFTER YOUR SESSION

Care for
your new ink.

Aftercare instructions from INKSUBWAY’s policy sheet.

01

Keep it clean

Wash your tattoo gently with mild, fragrance-free soap and lukewarm water.

02

Moisturize

Apply the recommended aftercare product as instructed by your artist.

03

Don’t touch

Do not scratch, pick, or peel the tattoo while it is healing.

04

Avoid soaking

Avoid swimming pools, beaches, hot tubs, and excessive moisture until the tattoo is fully healed.

05

Protect from sun

Protect your tattoo from direct sunlight and avoid tanning during the healing process.

06

Follow instructions

Follow all aftercare instructions carefully to ensure the best healing results.
