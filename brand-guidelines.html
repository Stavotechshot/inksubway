01 / Identity

A high-contrast serif I and S combine with a needle, sweeping line and four-point star. The serif wordmark and spaced TATTOO descriptor follow the supplied reference. Use the horizontal lockup for headers, stacked for square compositions and the icon for compact spaces. All SVG artwork uses vector paths; no logo font is required.

03 / Space & scale

Leave clear space equal to twice the I stem width on every side of the visible artwork, including around a lockup. Suggested minimum widths: detailed monogram 64 px / 18 mm; circular submark 72 px / 20 mm; horizontal 240 px / 65 mm; stacked 180 px / 50 mm. The fine needle and sweeping line require larger display sizes than the first draft. Inspect actual production proofs, especially at small sizes.

Scale uniformly. Do not stretch, rotate, add shadows, recolour individual letters or close the interweave gaps. Use a solid field behind the mark; avoid placing it over busy artwork.

04 / Typography & voice

Reference typography: Playfair Display for headings and Montserrat for body/UI. These font files were not supplied. This offline prototype uses Georgia and Arial as fallbacks; the path-based wordmark was rebuilt from installed Times New Roman letter outlines with custom spacing. It is an approximation pending typography review. No font installation is required to display the logos. Keep language personal, clear and calm. Identify Stephanie as the artist and owner; avoid invented experience, awards, location, specialties or client quotes.
